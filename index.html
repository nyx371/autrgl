RIFTFORGE

An automation roguelike.

  • Tap the rift to jolt energy. Chain taps for combos.
  • Tap the orbiting nodes to build machines. They run themselves.
  • Injectors charge the rift. 100% wins.
  • Tap the vent port to dump heat. Max heat = meltdown.
  • Draft cards. Stack tags.
