RIFTFORGE

An automation defense roguelike.

  • Enemies swarm in from the edges to eat your Spire.
  • Tap enemies to zap them. Chain hits for combos.
  • Tap corner nodes to build machines. They fight for you.
  • Tap the nova port to blast everything back.
  • Survive 10 waves. Draft cards between them.
